CLOUDY AI - HOW TO GET YOUR APK (6 steps)

After extracting this zip you must see exactly 5 things:
   .github          (folder)
   www              (folder)
   package.json
   capacitor.config.json
   START-HERE.txt

1. Go to github.com and open your cloudy-ai repository.

2. Click: Add file -> Upload files

3. Drag ALL 5 items above into the upload box at once.
   (Drag the .github folder and the www folder themselves, whole.)

4. Click the green "Commit changes" button.

5. Click the "Actions" tab at the top. A build called
   "Build APK" starts by itself. Wait for the green tick
   (about 5-8 minutes).

6. Click the finished run -> scroll down to "Artifacts" ->
   download "CloudyAI-APK". Unzip it -> inside is
   app-debug.apk -> send it to your phone -> tap -> Install.

Open the app: create your name + password, go to BOOTH,
load a beat, press RECORD. First time only, tap "Allow"
when it asks for the microphone.

If the upload box refuses the .github folder, tell Claude -
there is a 1-minute fallback.
